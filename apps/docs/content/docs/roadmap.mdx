## Event registry

A [hosted event registry](https://github.com/ThallesP/webhooks/issues/11). Publish your events, and consumers pull them as types with one command, instead of installing a package that exports [`EventUnion`](/docs/typed-payloads).

## Consumer SDK CLI

A [CLI to generate a branded consumer SDK](https://github.com/ThallesP/webhooks/issues/3) for the apps that receive your webhooks.

## More adapters

Outbox adapters for more databases and ORMs, next to the [Prisma adapter](/docs/prisma-adapter):

- [Drizzle](https://github.com/ThallesP/webhooks/issues/4)
- [Kysely](https://github.com/ThallesP/webhooks/issues/5)
- [TypeORM](https://github.com/ThallesP/webhooks/issues/6)
- [MikroORM](https://github.com/ThallesP/webhooks/issues/7)
- [Sequelize](https://github.com/ThallesP/webhooks/issues/8)
- [Knex](https://github.com/ThallesP/webhooks/issues/9)
- [MongoDB](https://github.com/ThallesP/webhooks/issues/10)

Until then, [write your own](/docs/write-an-adapter) in five functions.
